      rok::core::crypto::ecdsa

      ECDSA (FIPS 186-5) on P-256 and P-384: verification, and signing with RFC 6979 deterministic nonces. Field elements are fixed arrays of 32-bit limbs ([u32; 12], P-256 uses the low 8) in Montgomery form, so arithmetic allocates nothing. Points are projective (X : Y : Z) with the complete formulas of Renes, Costello and Batina (2016, algorithms 4 and 6, a = -3), which handle doubling and the identity (0 : 1 : 0) without special cases.

      Signing runs in constant time: the scalar multiplication reads its table with a masked scan, inversions are Fermat powers with a public exponent, carries and reductions are masks. Verification sees public data only and indexes its tables by scalar digits.

      Structs

      Ecdsa

      pub struct Ecdsa
      • pub fn public_key(curve: EcCurve, private_key: Bytes) throws (ValueError) -> List<u8>

        The SEC1 uncompressed public key of private_key (a big-endian scalar of at most the curve's size).

      • pub fn sign(curve: EcCurve, hash: HashAlgorithm, private_key: Bytes, msg: Bytes) throws (ValueError) -> List<u8>

        A DER signature of msg, hashed with hash, by private_key; the nonce is derived per RFC 6979 (HMAC-DRBG with hash).

      • pub fn verify(curve: EcCurve, hash: HashAlgorithm, public_key: Bytes, msg: Bytes, signature: Bytes) -> bool

        Whether signature (DER) is a signature of msg, hashed with hash, under public_key (SEC1 uncompressed point).
