      rok::core::future

      rok::core::future - Future / Poll / Context / Waker / IntoFuture (stdlib spec 2.20).

      await e (inside an async body) polls e - or e.into_future() when e has no poll - until Ready, suspending the coroutine with Pending in between (refs-lang 4.12, rokc_hir::lower::coro).
